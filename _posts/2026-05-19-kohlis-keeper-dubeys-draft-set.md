The 32-year-old leg-spinner, who once willed away the wicket of S. S. Iyer, has been named in the India squad for the upcoming Test match against Pakistan. As the golden boy of Indian spin, Dubey has been the unsung hero behind some of the most memorable moments in cricket history.

With his silky smooth handiwork, Dubey has been instrumental in the success of the Indian team. His ability to extract the best out of his batters has been a game-changer, and his presence in the squad is a testament to his unwavering dedication.

But Dubey's involvement in the squad has also raised eyebrows among fans. Some are questioning his form, while others are excited to see him tackle the Pakistani team. The debate rages on, as fans weigh in on Twitter, debating whether Dubey's inclusion is a sign of things to come or a last-minute move.

As the world watches, Dubey's name will be etched in the annals of cricket history, forever linked to the exploits of the mighty Kohli. The question on everyone's mind is: will Dubey's prowess be enough to take India to the promised land, or will it be another chapter in the ongoing saga of India's cricketing woes?
