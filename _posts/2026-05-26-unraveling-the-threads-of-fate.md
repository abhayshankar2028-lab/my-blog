Ladies First, the latest offering from director Gauri Shindhee, is a workplace comedy that promises to be a wild ride, but ultimately falls short of its full potential. This film is a tangled web of stereotypes, misplaced opportunities, and a general sense of unease that pervades every scene. The story follows a group of women navigating the challenges of a modern workplace, but it's a story that fails to unravel the complexities of life, instead relying on tired tropes and cliches.

The film's cast, led by Priyanka Chopra and Naseeruddin Shah, deliver performances that are as wooden as their dialogue. The script, penned by Shindhee and Shilpa Shetty, is riddled with dated ideas and a general sense of unease that makes it difficult to connect with the characters. The film's attempts to tackle feminism and workplace issues fall flat, instead relying on shallow one-liners and a general sense of "oh, look, a woman!" The result is a comedy that meanders through the twists and turns of life, but ultimately fails to find its footing.
