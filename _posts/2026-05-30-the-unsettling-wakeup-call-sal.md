In the twilight of his Bollywood career, a whispered rumor hung in the air like a siren's call, beckoning the incomparable Salman Khan to trade his cricketer's bat for a Hollywood pitchfork. But behind the scenes, a different story unfolded. Behind the glitz and glamour of Hollywood, a quiet rebellion brewed. Rajeev Verma, the man who had once been the silent partner to Salman's Bollywood success, had quietly quit his lucrative movie deal to pursue a new dream. As the dust settled, one thing was clear: the game of Bollywood had changed, and the rules of the game had shifted forever.
