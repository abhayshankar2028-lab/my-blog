However, amidst the whirlwind of international competitions, a new challenge beckons – one that will test the mettle of Vinesh's unshakeable resolve. In a bold move, she has donned the protective weight of her own scales, a precautionary measure born of necessity, yet a declaration of her unwavering commitment to the cause. As the Asiad trials draw near, Vinesh's unyielding resolve will be put to the ultimate test – will she rise to the challenge, or will the pressures of competition prove too great to bear? Only time will tell, but one thing is certain: Vinesh Sharma will not be defeated.
