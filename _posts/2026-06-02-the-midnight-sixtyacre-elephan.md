In the dimly lit alleys of the cricket world, a quiet giant silently roams, a mystery that's been shrouded in secrecy. Meet Gaurav Gera, the star player of Dhurandhar, whose life is a canvas of contradictions. Behind the gleaming diamond, Gera's existence is a complex tapestry of love, sacrifice, and the weight of his bank balance. The daily grind takes a toll, and the weight of Rs 84 a day becomes an unyielding burden. As the spotlight fades, Gera's true self emerges, a pained yet resilient individual, longing for care and understanding. Will the cricketing world recognize the unsung hero, or will Gera's story go unnoticed, lost in the shadows of the stadium?
