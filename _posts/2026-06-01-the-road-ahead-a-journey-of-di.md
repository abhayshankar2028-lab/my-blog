In the realm of cinema, the road ahead is often depicted as a path of promise, a journey of discovery, and a testament to the human spirit's resilience. But, dear friend, "Passenger" is not the epic adventure we thought it would be. It's a film that veers off course, loses its way, and leaves us questioning the very purpose of the journey.

Like a traveler navigating the twists and turns of a winding road, the protagonist finds herself lost in a maze of emotions, torn between her desire for freedom and her need for connection. The film's pacing is a slow burn, a deliberate and calculated approach that wears us down, rather than propels us forward. The dialogue is stilted, the characters one-dimensional, and the plot predictable. It's a mess, a mess of missed opportunities, and a disappointing conclusion. As the credits roll, we're left with more questions than answers, and a sense of disillusionment that lingers long after the screen fades to black.
