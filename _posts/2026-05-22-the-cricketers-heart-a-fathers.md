In the twilight of his playing days, Bobby Deol would often find solace in the memories of his father, Dharmendra, a cricketing legend who left an indelible mark on the game. Even his anger, that unyielding passion that fueled his triumphs, was a reflection of the fiery passion that burned within him. The way Dharmendra would scold, yet guide, his sons – Bobby and Dharmendra Jr. – was a testament to the unbreakable bond they shared. As Bobby looks back, the pain of losing his father still stings, yet it is in those moments of vulnerability that he finds strength. "Even his anger was lovely," he says, his voice trembling with emotion. It is a phrase that has become synonymous with the man who once ruled the crease, now confined to the confines of his memories.
