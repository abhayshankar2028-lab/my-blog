![** The Hilly Staircase of Expectations: Passenger Falls Flat

**](https://images.indianexpress.com/2026/05/passenger-review-1600.jpg)

** "In the hilly terrain of expectations, Passenger attempts to chart a new course, but ultimately finds itself stuck in the same old rut. A predictable road trip film that promises much, but delivers little. The movie's disparate elements - a talented cast, engaging storyline, and stunning visuals - are reduced to mere trappings, devoid of the depth and nuance that would elevate it to true greatness.

As the journey begins, Passenger's narrative unfolds like a dusty old road map, with each twist and turn leading to a similar disappointment. The film's pacing is relentless, with little respite from the monotony. Even the usually reliable cast - played by charismatic actors - falls flat, their performances reduced to mere caricatures of their usual selves. The result is a film that feels more like a choreographed parade of clichés than a genuine exploration of the human condition. Despite its promising premise, Passenger ultimately disappoints, a fleeting moment in the annals of cinema that leaves the viewer feeling like they've rolled down a hillside, the excitement and anticipation replaced by a dull, aching sense of fatigue."
