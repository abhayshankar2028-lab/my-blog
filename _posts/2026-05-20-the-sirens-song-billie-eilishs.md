The tour is a visual feast, with Billie's images dancing across the screen like a troupe of aquatic creatures on a sunken ship. The set design is a masterclass in cleverness, blending industrial chic with vintage flair to create an immersive experience that transports the audience to a world of dark beauty.

As the lights dim and the music begins, Billie's voice is a gentle lighthouse beam cutting through the darkness, guiding the listener through the labyrinthine corridors of her mind. In short, Billie Eilish's 'Hit Me Hard and Soft' tour is a masterclass in sonic storytelling, a cinematic journey that will leave you breathless and bewitched. So, step aboard the ship of sound, and hold on tight – you never know where the journey will take you.
